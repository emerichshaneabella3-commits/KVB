Happy birthday, my Keia! I hope you know how much you are loved, especially by me. I don't think I'll ever get tired of reminding you how amazing you are and how proud I am of you. Not just because of the things you achieve or the things you do, but simply because of who you are. I know life can get really draining sometimes, and I know there are days when things don't go the way you want them to. But I hope you never lose yourself along the way. I hope you always find little reasons to keep going, rediscover your purpose, and find happiness in the simplest things. I hope you find peace even in the things you cannot control. Alam ko din how much you try to be there for everyone, how you make sure the people around you are okay, and how you always try to give what you can. You don't always have to be the strong one, baby. You don't always have to figure everything out on your own. Let me be there for you, too. Let me take care of you on the days you don't know how to take care of yourself. You can rest with me, you can be vulnerable with me, and you never have to pretend that everything is okay when it isn't. I really want to be someone you can come home to, even on your hardest days. I hope you get everything your heart has been quietly wishing for, not just today but in all the years ahead of you. You're allowed to take your time. You're allowed to figure things out at your own pace. And please, never think that you are difficult to love just because you have bad days or because sometimes you don't know what to do with yourself. I love you in your happiest moments, but I also want to love you through the days when you feel like you're not at your best. Always remember that you will always have me cheering for you. Even when you feel like you're not making progress, I'll be proud of how far you've come. Thank you for being you, baby. I'm so lucky that I get to know you, love you, and witness all the versions of you that life has yet to bring. Thank you for existing, beb. Happy birthday again, baby ko. I love you always, in the little things, in the big things, and even in the moments when you forget how lovable you are. More birthdays together, baby. Sorry if this is the only gift I can give for now. I know hindi lang ito yung deserve mo, because you deserve everything good in this world and so much more. Bawi na lang ako kapag medyo malapit ka na. I can't wait for the day na hindi na lang through a screen yung celebrations natin. GOOD HEALTH, BABY HA! I hope this new chapter brings you more happiness, peace, opportunities, and reasons to smile. I love you, my Keia. Always. — YOUR FAVORITE PERSON FROM THE PHILIPPINES ❤️
